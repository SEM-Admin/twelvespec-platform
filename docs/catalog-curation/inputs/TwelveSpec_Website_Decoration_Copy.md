# Decoration & Customization Options
**Made to Spec. Built to Impress.**

Your brand should look right on every product. TwelveSpec helps you select the decoration method that suits your artwork, product material, desired finish, quantity and timeline.

## Shop by product type

### Apparel and uniforms
Choose direct screen printing for many matching shirts, DTG or DTF for detailed full-color designs and smaller runs, and embroidery for polished uniforms. Performance fabrics require specialized transfers and testing.

### Custom hats and headwear
Flat embroidery is a classic choice. 3D puff adds a bold raised effect; embroidered, woven, leather and rubber patches create distinctive looks. Not every hat construction supports every option.

### Bags and accessories
Canvas totes lend themselves to screen printing. Packs and work bags often look best embroidered or with patches. Lightweight polyester bags may suit special heat transfers.

### Drinkware and kitchen
Laser engraving creates a permanent-feeling premium mark on suitable metal or coated tumblers; spot-color printing, full-color digital printing, glass etching and sublimation-coated items offer other looks.

### Promotional products and gifts
Plastic pens, tech gifts, plaques and desk items each have specific materials and imprint areas. Available methods include pad print, UV digital, laser, debossing and custom molded emblems.

### Trade shows and displays
Fabric table throws, flags, tents and tension displays often use dye-sublimation printing. Retractable banners and rigid display panels can use compatible wide-format UV graphics.

### Signs, decals and graphics
Make an impact with full-color banners, yard signs, window graphics, vehicle magnets and adhesive decals. We match the ink, film, laminate and installation needs to the use environment.

### Marketing print and packaging
Business cards, brochures, catalogs, mailers, labels and custom boxes can be produced digitally or on press, with specialty options such as foil, embossing, spot gloss and die cutting.

## How do we choose the right method?
We consider your product material, placement, logo detail, colors, order quantity, feel, intended use, budget and timeline. The available decoration options and imprint areas are confirmed for each specific item before production.

## Common questions

**Can I get flat embroidery on a hat?** Yes. Flat embroidery is a standard option on many caps and beanies. Structured hats may also support 3D puff embroidery.

**What is the difference between screen printing and DTF?** Screen printing applies ink through screens and often wins on matching designs at volume. DTF uses a digitally printed film transfer and is convenient for full-color or shorter runs. Screen-printed heat transfers are another option; they are not the same as DTF.

**Can you print on a beige shirt using sublimation?** Traditional dye sublimation works best on white or light-colored polyester; the shirt color changes the printed colors because there is no white ink. For precise artwork colors on beige blanks, we often review other methods.

**Can you print all over a polo?** Yes, through eligible custom cut-and-sew manufacturing or appropriate all-over printing services. This differs from placing a transfer on a finished blank polo.

**Can the same logo go on shirts, hats, cups and banners?** Usually yes, but artwork must be adapted for each method and imprint area; proofing requirements may differ.

**What if I do not know which option to choose?** Tell us the product, quantity, budget, timeline and intended look. We will recommend suitable methods and provide a proof for your approval.
