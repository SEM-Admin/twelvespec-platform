## Custom Ink

**Documented behavior.** Browser design and artist review accompany ordering; group forms collect participant sizes and payments. [S01: Custom Ink: how it works](https://www.customink.com/how-it-works), [S02: Collect sizes and payments with a group form](https://www.customink.com/help_center/collecting-sizes-and-payment-with-gof)

**Pattern to learn.** Reduce organizer effort while preserving a single accountable ordering workflow.

**Original TwelveSpec design.** Use one shared design project, an organizer view, a size/variant matrix and an explicit campaign close. Create a reviewed batch request at close; keep participant payment states separate from the production order.

**Boundary.** TwelveSpec must still bind every proof to the exact variant and obtain its own production authorization. Group fund settlement remains reserved.

**Unverified information.** Internal design engine, artwork algorithms, group settlement implementation and private APIs were not inspected.

**Related designs:** [F01](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f01), [F02](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f02), [F05](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f05), [F08](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f08), [F11](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f11), [F33](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f33), [F34](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f34).

## PCNA

**Documented behavior.** PCNA accepts authorized distributor applications and advertises decorated goods across apparel and hardgoods. [S03: PCNA authorized distributor account application](https://cloud.engage.pcna.com/account_application), [S04: PCNA print and promo marketing page](https://cloud.engage.pcna.com/Print-Promo-Marketing)

**Pattern to learn.** Represent an external source as a supplier offer with decoration and fulfillment capabilities, rather than replacing the canonical product.

**Original TwelveSpec design.** Store source product/part IDs, available methods, imprint areas, charge identifiers and fulfillment origins. Make account eligibility and service verification separate administrator steps.

**Boundary.** An expired marketing offer supplies no usable margin, order volume, current rush promise or entitlement.

**Unverified information.** Main site fetch returned 403. Exact supported services, API profiles, negotiated costs and media rights require authorized documents.

**Related designs:** [F03](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f03), [F04](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f04), [F13](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f13), [F16](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f16), [F29](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f29).

## SanMar

**Documented behavior.** Public integration choices include PromoStandards, flat files and direct services; brand and marketplace restrictions are published. [S06: SanMar electronic integration offerings](https://www.sanmar.com/resources/electronicintegration/integrationofferings), [S07: SanMar terms and conditions](https://info.sanmar.com/termsandconditions)

**Pattern to learn.** Separate the blank garment, warehouse inventory and decorating operation; publication rights vary by channel.

**Original TwelveSpec design.** Model style/color/size variants, warehouse-level inventory snapshots and blank-to-decorator job routing. Add channel restrictions to the publication gate and mapping provenance to every imported field.

**Boundary.** Website access, API access, brand image permission and marketplace authorization are distinct.

**Unverified information.** Account cost tiers, freight, exact service versions, inventory reservation and decorator agreements remain unverified.

**Related designs:** [F02](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f02), [F03](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f03), [F16](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f16), [F17](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f17), [F18](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f18), [F29](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f29), [F35](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f35).

## Imprint.com

**Documented behavior.** Pricing depends on product, imprint colors, quantity and method; digital proofs are conditional rather than universal. [S08: Imprint.com catalog and design assistance](https://imprint.com/), [S09: Imprint.com pricing factors](https://imprint.com/help-center/faq-how-is-my-price-determined), [S10: Imprint.com proof eligibility](https://imprint.com/help-center/will-i-get-a-proof-to-review-before-my-order-is-printed)

**Pattern to learn.** A short guided configurator can expose complexity progressively and route exceptions to an assisted quote.

**Original TwelveSpec design.** Ask method-dependent questions, update quantity-break comparisons and show the cost assumptions behind an estimate. Keep visual preview, proof review and production preflight as separate states.

**Boundary.** Selected-product shipping/setup offers cannot become global TwelveSpec pricing rules. Preserve the handoff’s stronger proof requirements.

**Unverified information.** No public technical API contract was established in the sampled sources; internal pricing and artwork processing are unknown.

**Related designs:** [F01](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f01), [F02](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f02), [F04](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f04), [F05](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f05), [F07](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f07), [F25](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f25).

## 4imprint

**Pattern to learn.** Pair customer self-service with an accountable rep and a clear queue for decisions.

**Original TwelveSpec design.** Attach an account owner, task timeline, proof decision and promised date to each quote/order. Track artwork waiting time separately from supplier production and carrier transit.

**Boundary.** Vendor credit and cancellation policies do not override TwelveSpec prepaid/approved Net 15 policy.

**Unverified information.** No private CRM, sourcing agreements, unit economics or API implementation was inspected.

## Printify

**Documented behavior.** The documented catalog distinguishes blueprint, provider and variant; routing can choose exact or similar alternatives within settings. [S12: Printify API reference](https://developers.printify.com/), [S13: Printify Order Routing behavior](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483625090321-What-is-Order-Routing-and-how-does-it-work)

**Pattern to learn.** A purchasable variant includes the fulfillment provider and print-area contract, with explicit substitution policy.

**Original TwelveSpec design.** Build a provider-neutral adapter and routing proposal. Before a source change, compare exact item, print geometry, region, landed cost and promised date; require renewed authorization for material changes.

**Boundary.** Autonomous supplier spend remains Coming Soon. A similar product is never an invisible replacement for an approved item.

**Unverified information.** Provider-specific quality, charges, geography, token scopes and observed fulfillment performance require account testing.

**Related designs:** [F04](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f04), [F18](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f18), [F19](FEATURE_PACKS.md#f19).

## OrderMyGear

**Documented behavior.** Company, pop-up, redemption and POD stores are advertised, with budgets and permissions; the operating dealer handles fulfillment and refunds. [S14: OrderMyGear online store types and controls](https://www.ordermygear.com/online-stores/), [S15: OrderMyGear FAQs and fulfillment responsibility](https://www.ordermygear.com/faqs/)

**Pattern to learn.** Store programs are policy-driven purchasing contexts with different close, funding and fulfillment behavior.

**Original TwelveSpec design.** Create explicit program types, membership/eligibility, budgets, brand approvals and close dates. Carry store and allowance identity through cart, request, quote, order and cancellation.

**Boundary.** Points, fundraising allocations and real financial settlement must not be represented as interchangeable money.

**Unverified information.** Plan entitlements, API schemas and customer-specific commercial rights remain unverified.

## PrintNow

**Documented behavior.** Enterprise documentation separates REST resources, webhooks and cXML/procurement integration; order operations include print-file retrieval and shipping updates. [S16: PrintNow Enterprise API introduction](https://docs.printnow.com/docs/enterprise-api/introduction), [S17: PrintNow public integration overview](https://printnow.com/web-to-print-api)

**Pattern to learn.** The editor, storefront, production files and order management need stable contracts rather than a visual-only connection.

**Boundary.** Current Product API descriptions are narrower than marketing language about a full print catalog. Packaging CAD and validated 3D remain future.

**Unverified information.** Internal rendering technology, template file format, license rights and tenant-specific endpoint access are unknown.

## Printful

**Pattern to learn.** Separate an editable order draft from the financially consequential production submission.

**Original TwelveSpec design.** Use exact provider variants and print files, a verified pre-submit gate, cost estimates, idempotency and shipment updates. Preserve supplier payable events separately from customer receipt/payout events.

**Boundary.** Pin the tested API version and capability. A successful catalog read does not verify production submission.

**Unverified information.** Authorized account configuration, current region pricing, samples, branding options and actual API behavior remain untested.

## Oracle: NetSuite and Fusion ERP/SCM
